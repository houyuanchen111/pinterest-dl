# Wall Projection Variation Lexicon

Use these alternatives to create controlled variations of the standalone wall-projection prompts. Replace only dimensions that remain compatible with the prompt's subject and explicit constraints.

## Incident Direction

- from the upper left toward the lower right
- from the upper right toward the lower left
- from the left edge at a shallow downward angle
- from the right edge at a low upward angle

## Light and Shadow Edges

- crisp and clearly defined edges
- medium-soft edges
- soft feathered edges
- strongly diffused edges
- a mixture of sharp and softly blurred layers

## Light and Time

- cool morning daylight
- neutral midday sunlight
- soft overcast daylight
- warm late-afternoon sunlight
- golden-hour sunlight
- pale moonlit blue-white light

## Flat Background Materials

- warm ivory limewash wall
- pale-gray smooth concrete surface
- muted beige mineral-plaster wall
- desaturated terracotta matte-clay surface
- warm-gray handmade-paper-like wall
- off-white fine plaster wall
- flat pale limestone surface

## Common Restrictions

Only the flat surface and projected light or shadow. No visible light source, no physical window or plant, no furniture, no objects, no people, no floor line, no architectural edges, no text, no logo, no watermark.
